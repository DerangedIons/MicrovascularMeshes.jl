      85              : 
      86              : Unlike [`generate_network`](@ref) it is pure (no rng) and small, and every count, radius,

      88              : [`smooth_network`](@ref), which supplies the curvature when composed with it.
      89              : 
      90              : Along `x`, a trunk takes the first 15% of `extent`, the arterial fan the next 25%, and the

      93              : leaves. The capillary bed is a ladder — arterial leaf `k` feeds venous leaves `k` and

      95              : network is symmetric under a half-turn about the bed center rather than a pure `x`-mirror.

     100              : junction; the leaves at the two ends of each ladder feed only one capillary instead of two.
