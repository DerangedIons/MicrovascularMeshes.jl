      15              :  4. Feeding arteriole(s) and a draining venule are appended and connected to the capillary

      18              :     [`prune_disconnected`](@ref)).

      21              : [`HalfSphere`](@ref) the footprint is a disk and the mesh fills a dome whose feeding
      22              : vessels pierce the curved surface.
      23              : 
      24              : Pass an `rng` for reproducible output.
      25              : 
      26              : ### Examples
